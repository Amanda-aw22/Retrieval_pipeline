# INPUT (method B — sectioned, labeled passages)

You are given passages from ONE research paper. References and
acknowledgements have been removed. The passages are numbered and labeled, e.g.
`[Passage 12 | page 5 | Results | 3.2 Mechanical properties | cites prior work]`.
The label tells you the page, the section, the subheading, whether the passage
is a TABLE, and whether it contains citation markers. Use the labels to decide
whose material a value belongs to (§4), and put the passage number of each
quote in `passage`.
